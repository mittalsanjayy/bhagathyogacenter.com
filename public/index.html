Good questions, answered

Can I join if I’m new to yoga?

You’re welcome to ask about getting started. Let the studio know it’s your first class so they can help you find a suitable option.

What should I wear?

Choose comfortable clothes that let you move freely. If you’re unsure about footwear or anything else, ask when you get in touch.

Should I bring a mat?

Mat and prop availability hasn’t been confirmed. Please check with the studio before your first visit.

How do I reserve a place?

Call or WhatsApp to ask about current availability. This website doesn’t take bookings or confirm places.

What if I have an injury or health concern?

Speak with a qualified healthcare professional first, then contact the studio and tell the instructor about any relevant movement considerations.
